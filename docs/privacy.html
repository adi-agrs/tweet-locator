RecallX AI ("the extension") is designed to be fully private by default. This policy explains exactly what data the extension interacts with and how it's handled.

What data is collected

While you browse Twitter/X, the extension automatically captures the following for tweets that appear on your screen:

  • Tweet text
  • Author name
  • Tweet permalink (URL)
  • Timestamp of when it was captured

Where this data is stored

All captured data is stored locally on your device, using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. RecallX AI does not operate any server, does not have a backend, and does not transmit any of your data over the network at any point.

Data sharing

RecallX AI does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. There are no analytics, trackers, or ad SDKs included in the extension.

Data retention

Captured tweets are automatically deleted after 60 days, and history is capped at 10,000 tweets, whichever limit is reached first. You can also export your full history as a JSON file or clear it at any time by removing the extension.

Optional AI semantic search

RecallX AI includes this optional companion website for semantic (meaning-based) search. If you choose to use it, you manually export your history as a JSON file from the extension and upload it here yourself. All processing — including running the embedding model — happens locally in your browser using Transformers.js. The uploaded file is never sent to a server; it exists only in your browser's memory for that session.

Permissions justification

  • storage — required to save your tweet history locally on your device.
  • activeTab / tabs — required to open the history page when you click the extension icon.
  • host permissions (x.com, twitter.com) — required so the content script can detect and read tweets as you scroll, which is the extension's core function.
